## 0. Executive summary

This pass fixes **one family of defects: everywhere the product said the Federation knew
something about a visitor, it was reading `localStorage`.**

The site's primary conversion surface — "Authorize Connection Sequence" on the home page —
enrolled nobody. It refused to open without a connected crypto wallet (SOP Law 4 makes
wallet linking optional), collected a signature, sent a transaction to the treasury Safe
calling a function no deployed contract implements, wrote an alias to the browser, and then
announced `[ MUSTER_SIGNATURE_SUCCESSFUL ]` and displayed a rank the Federation had never
issued. The `members` table held zero rows throughout.

Two consequences mattered beyond the form itself:

- **Ranks were self-service.** Connecting a wallet promoted the browser to Deckhand
  (F39), committing a local draft promoted it to Navigator, and 500 locally-clicked
  reputation points made it a Guild Master. Those labels were rendered as
  "Clearance" in the Passport dialog, in the Armory, and gated `/policies` signalling,
  `/propose` submission and restricted provisions.
- **The Passport dialog ignored the Passport.** It subscribed to Supabase auth events only
  to `console.info` them, so a completed Email OTP sign-in changed nothing visible, and the
  dialog's identity block was populated from the browser's draft (F38).

The treasury's headline number had the same shape (F43): the "Vault Balance" called a
`getBalance()` view that a Safe multisig does not implement, so the read reverted on every
load and the panel rendered `0.00 ETH` — a figure it could not have obtained.

**Production has still not moved (F26).** `pirateusa.org` continues to serve
`assets/index-CXPQaP1L.js` from the 2026-10-25 build, with the wildcard `connect-src` and
`img-src https://*`. Three maintenance passes are stacked in an unmerged, green PR.

## 2. Fixed in this pass

| # | Sev | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| F37 | S1 | **The muster form enrolled nobody, and only opened for wallet holders.** `MusterRoll.jsx` covered itself with an `[ ENCRYPTION KEY REQUIRED. CONNECT WALLET … ]` overlay, then signed a message, sent `submitMusterSignature(bytes)` to `VITE_APF_TREASURY_ADDRESS` (a Safe, which implements no such function), and wrote `status: 'committed'` to `localStorage`. `members` was never written. Law 4 (Email OTP / optional wallet) and Law 1 both broken on the home page's primary CTA. | New `src/lib/api/members.js`: the signed-in member's own row is read (`fetchMember`) and written (`enrollMember`, upsert on `id = auth.uid()`, RLS-enforced). `MusterRoll.jsx` is now Passport-first: anonymous visitors are asked for a Passport instead of being told they enrolled; a connected wallet is offered as an **optional** link that must sign proof of control, and declining the signature files the record without it. The invented on-chain call, its telemetry and the fake success toast are gone. After filing, the page renders the record it just wrote, from the row. |
| F38 | S2 | **The Passport dialog displayed a rank from `localStorage`, not the session.** It also subscribed to `onAuthStateChange` only to log the event, so an OTP sign-in left the dialog showing the sign-in form forever. | `App.jsx` now boots the Passport once and re-reads it on `SIGNED_IN`/`SIGNED_OUT`/`TOKEN_REFRESHED`/`USER_UPDATED` through `refreshPassport()` in the store. The dialog renders the signed-in email and the member row it owns (alias, rank, guild, linked wallet), states `[ NOT ON THE MUSTER ROLL ]` when the account has no row, offers sign-out, and reports an unconfigured build instead of a dead submit button. |
| F39 | S2 | **Ranks were assigned by the browser.** `updateMusterRoll` promoted to Deckhand on wallet connect and to Navigator on a local `committed` status; `signalPolicy`, `registerSignal`, `addReputation` and `spendReputation` promoted to Guild Master above 500 local points. `userRole`/`guildAlignment` were persisted, and displayed as "Clearance". | All role escalation removed from `useAppStore`. `userRole`/`guildAlignment` are now mirrors of `members.role` / `members.guild`, written only by `refreshPassport`. Persisted state is versioned to 2 with a migration that deletes any previously self-assigned rank. `/shop`, `/propose` and `PolicyCard` gate on that mirrored rank, and the copy says rank is assigned by the Federation and cannot be raised locally. |
| F40 | S2 | **`/policies` claimed a signal was "CRYPTOGRAPHICALLY SECURED" after discarding the signature.** `handleAuthorize` asked the wallet to sign an "Authorize APF Directive Consensus" message, never used or stored it, then toasted the claim. | The signature ritual is removed. A signal is what it is — a browser-local expression of support — and the toast now says `[ SIGNAL RECORDED IN THIS BROWSER — NOT A FEDERATION BALLOT ]`. The published consensus score still does not move when a visitor signals. |
| F41 | S3 | **Dead control on all fourteen policy cards.** "Review Protocol ›" was a `<button>` with no handler; the `/policies` copy told visitors to "Upgrade clearance to Signal Consensus", a path that does not exist. | The slot now carries the protocol's constitutional anchor (`Anchored: 4th Amendment`), which is real doctrine metadata, and the copy states that no clearance level can be raised from a browser. |
| F42 | S3 | **`/events` RSVP claimed a secured signal that was thrown away.** `FleetMuster.jsx` signed a message, discarded it, wrote the event title to `localStorage` and toasted `[ EVENT SIGNAL CRYPTOGRAPHICALLY SECURED ]` — although `event_signals` exists, is RLS-scoped to the member, and was never written. | New `src/lib/api/eventSignals.js` inserts `{ event_id, member_id }` for the signed-in member; `UNIQUE(event_id, member_id)` is treated as "already registered". The button requires a member record, not a wallet, and the toast states where the signal went. |
| F43 | S1 | **The treasury "Vault Balance" could never have been a reading.** `useAPFContract` called `useReadContract(… "function getBalance() view returns (uint256)")` against the Safe address. A Safe proxy has no such selector, so the call reverted on every load and the hook's defensive fallback printed `0.00 ETH`; `/ledger` printed `0.00`. A number that is always wrong is worse than an honest absence. | The hook now reads the address's **native balance** with `getWalletBalance` (public RPC, no wallet connection required) and returns `null` — not `"0.00"` — when the address is unset, the read times out, or it fails. The home panel renders `[ TREASURY ADDRESS NOT PUBLISHED ]` / `[ BALANCE UNAVAILABLE ]`, and `/ledger` renders "Not published" / "Unavailable". |

Also removed: `console.info("[ MUSTER_HOOK_STAGED ]")` from the render body of `MusterRoll`
(dead instrumentation), and the now-unused `ethers` import in `TheTreasury` (the only use was
formatting a balance that never existed).
