### 2.1 Design decision worth recording

Adding two top-level nav links would have re-broken F18: the bar has a hard one-row budget at
1280 px and was already within ~12 px of it. The instruments therefore live behind a single
`Tools` control (native `<details>`, so keyboard and screen-reader behaviour is inherited),
`Direct Democracy` was renamed `Governance` to match the SOP and reclaim ~55 px, and the bar
spacing tightened to `gap-2`. The new test measures every control's bottom edge against the
64 px header rather than trusting a screenshot.
